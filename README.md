### 📜 Published Indian Patent Application

> **Patent Title:** *Multi-Hazard Prediction System Using Adaptive Terrain Graph*  
> **Application No:** `202641065384` · **Published in Official Patent Journal:** June 2026 (Filed: April 2026)  
> **Institution:** Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore  
> **Field:** Geospatial AI, Environmental Digital Twins & Computational Hydrology  
> 🔗 **Resources:** [📄 Read Official Patent Document (PDF)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/srikar123s/portfolio/main/public/multi-hazard.pdf) · [💻 GitHub Patent Implementation](https://github.com/srikar123s/Multi-Hazard-Prediction-System)

Formulated a novel software-defined Environmental Digital Twin that dynamically couples flood inundation ($F_R$) and landslide susceptibility ($L_R$) over an adaptive quadtree terrain mesh. Evaluated across 3 major geological basins (Western Ghats, Assam, and Uttarakhand), slashing raster processing overhead by **65%** and achieving sub-second hazard contour generation ($<1.0\text{s}$).
